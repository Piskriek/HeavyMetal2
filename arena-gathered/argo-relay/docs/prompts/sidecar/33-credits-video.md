# TASK-33 — Real-Time In-Engine Credits Music Video ("Four Floats" v4b @ 144 BPM)

> **File Destination in Repo:** `docs/prompts/sidecar/33-credits-video.md`
> **Source Plan:** `docs/argo/plans/30-credits-video.md` & `docs/argo/plans/26b-ending-song-v2.md`
> **Target Branch:** `main` (Post-`TASK-24` Polish Wave)
> **Audio Assets:**
> - Main Ending Track: `1.2x` time-stretched (pitch-kept) **v4b** at **144 BPM** (`~117 s` vocal run)
> - Instrumental Loop Metadata: `outro.json` generated by `scripts/music-outro.py` (`loopStart`, `loopEnd`, `bpm: 144`)
> - Bonus Lab Monitor Replay Track: `1.2x` pitch-up version titled **`"Four Floats (Overclocked)"`**

## Context & Goal

Build a real-time, deterministic in-engine credits music video in `apps/web/src/credits/` that plays automatically upon completing Act IX (`A9-03`) and can be replayed anytime from the Main Menu (`[Credits]`) or the Lab's `old-monitor`. Following the modular code-driven chapter architecture of [JohnHeibel/PDoomVideo](https://github.com/JohnHeibel/PDoomVideo/blob/main/README.md) and the split telemetry layout of [Valve's Portal Credits](https://developer.valvesoftware.com/wiki/Credits.txt), each chapter is a standalone TypeScript module on a shared `144 BPM` timeline (`1 bar = 1.6667 s`, `1 beat = 0.4167 s`) that drives our live Three.js Lab and Lunar Plot scenes through all seven visual stages (`Stage 0a` Wireframe $\to$ `Stage 6` Suit-Cam PBR) and all six `data-fidelity` HUD skins (`Plan 28` / `Brief 25`).

### 2. Audio Clock, `outro.json` Loop & Lab Monitor `"Four Floats (Overclocked)"` Replay

1. **Primary Sync Clock (`timeline.ts`):**
   - Drive `t = audioContext.currentTime - startTime` at **144 BPM** (`0.4167 s` per beat, `1.6667 s` per 4/4 bar). If audio is muted or unavailable in headless tests, fall back seamlessly to `(performance.now() - startMs) / 1000`.
2. **Seamless Instrumental Chorus Loop (`outro.json` from `scripts/music-outro.py`):**
   - Load `outro.json` (`{ bpm: 144, outroStart, loopStart, loopEnd }`).
   - When the vocal track reaches `"Sector Four"` (`outroStart`, `~1:57`), transition to **Chapter 7 (`ch7-outro-loop.ts`)** and loop the Web Audio `AudioBufferSourceNode` seamlessly between `loopStart` and `loopEnd` on the exact 144 BPM bar boundary until the player clicks `[Return to Sandbox]` or presses `Esc`.
3. **Lab `old-monitor` Jukebox Replay (`apps/web/src/play/lab-room.ts`):**
   - After Act IX is completed (or via a Studio/sandbox toggle), interacting with the permanent `old-monitor` fixture in the Lab offers two replay actions:
     - `[Play Ending Credits: "Four Floats" (v4b)]` — launches the full in-engine credits sequence.
     - `[Play "Four Floats (Overclocked)"]` — plays the `1.2x` pitch-shifted chiptune/vocal version through the Lab monitor speaker (`World_SFX_Bus`) while the player continues working in the Lab, pulsing a 4-color (`#ff3d8a`, `#7cff4d`, `#ffc13d`, `#3dc8ff`) equalizer on the monitor screen.

- **Lunar Sky:** Always render the **Moon's real sky** (`@hm/lighting` / `crafter/world.ts` starfield, Rayleigh horizon band, directional sun, and Stage 3–6 clouds)—never render the Goblin planet in the lunar sky.
