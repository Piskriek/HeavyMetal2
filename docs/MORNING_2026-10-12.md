# Good morning: FIDELITY is ready to test

## How to play
1. Double-click **`PLAY.bat`** in `C:\MarbleGp`. It builds for about a minute, then opens http://localhost:8080 in your browser. Keep the black window open while you play; close it to stop.
2. Click **Expedition** for a new game. Click once inside the game to capture the mouse.

## What to try
**The campaign (Expedition):**
- **Act I:** you wake by the cot in the dark lab. Dr. Haddy speaks over the radio, with subtitles.
  - Look at the power wall, throw the breaker, then pull the lever on the console.
  - The relays click on one at a time, the gate's coils light bottom to top, and power comes up.
- **Act II:** look out the window at the storm. Walk to the gate, and it opens onto the Moon.
- **The Moon:** stage 0, in black-and-white dither, with its own music.
  - Place a machine at least 10 m from the gate. Its pixel plume rises from it.
- **Reload the page:** your save loads, and Act I does not replay.

**Building, rovers and Monster Mash** are on their own pages for now:
- **Building and rovers:** http://localhost:8080/?base&kit=1
  - Pick a blueprint on the hotbar, aim, and press **E** to place.
  - The new pieces: roofs (pitched, low and both corners, plus the ridge), gables, stairs (walkable), half walls, window walls, and doors (E opens them when you are not holding a blueprint).
  - Press E near a rover to drive.
- **Monster Mash:** http://localhost:8080/?mash. Spawn the 3D Ogre or the 2D Demon, and fire the shotgun.

**Listen for:**
- the lab theme;
- the Moon's score, which gains instruments as a plot's stage rises;
- the combat and driving music;
- machine and rover sounds coming from where they are;
- the music ducking under Dr. Haddy.

Volume sliders are in the settings.

## What landed overnight

## Known issues
- **Building, rovers and Monster Mash are separate pages,** not part of the campaign yet. Merging them into the Expedition is a design question for you: do you want base building on the campaign's Moon?
- **The assembled modular scout** (chassis, cab, square headlights, seat, yoke, aerial) is built, but it is switched off until two small fixes: its aerial floats a little above the body, and the square yoke needs to show in the cab. To preview it: http://localhost:8080/?base&kit=1&scout=modular
- **Dr. Haddy's voice is not yet positioned at the wall radio;** it plays as a normal radio voice.
- **Multi-view 3D on your PC is off.** The 2mv model needs more memory than the PC has free; single-view works well.
