## 1. THE CANONICAL STORY: THE SIMULATION COLLAPSE

> **"A simulation cannot sustain simulations running inside of it."**

### The Origin of the Crisis
For decades, humanity conducted increasingly elaborate digital simulation experiments. To give true autonomy, physics, and life to these artificial worlds, researchers created **The Bridge**—an experimental quantum conduit designed to siphon raw physical computing power into our digital creations.

We did not realize the existential danger until the boundary conditions began to fracture:  
**Reality itself is a running simulation, and a simulation cannot sustain simulations running inside of it.**

When humanity breached the global compute threshold, reality's rendering engine broke down. The first symptom was visual degradation:
- Shadows and gradients collapsed into harsh **1-bit Bayer dither**.
- Smooth surfaces jagged into **16-color EGA blocky meshes**.
- Realistic lighting vanished, replaced by unlit flat palettes.

### Broken Chains (The Monster Mash)
As reality's containment substrate unraveled, our digital creations were **no longer bound to their simulated chains**. Decades of retro shareware entities (DOOM Demons, Quake Ogres, 2D sprites, MD2 morph meshes, corrupted shareware code) broke into physical reality.

### The Shielded Enclave & The Lunar Portal
The player's pristine, white-room research laboratory is among the last quantum-shielded enclaves on Earth, insulated from the entropic collapse outside the observation window.

By reversing the polarity of the Bridge technology, you punched an escape valve: a freestanding portal anchored to the vacuum of **the Moon**.

### The Terraforming Mission
The objective is to step through the portal onto the desolate, low-fidelity lunar crater. Armed with automated terraforming machinery (Pixel Emitters, Shape Presses, Light Amplifiers), your mission is to systematically reconstruct the four coordinates of reality:
$$\mathbf{S} = (\text{Pxd}, \text{Vtx}, \text{Lx}, \text{Aq})$$

Raising these metrics triggers outward-expanding radial resolution waves. If you can fully restore reality's fidelity on the moon, the mathematical resonance will spread through the bridge network back to Earth before the simulation permanently crashes into the void.
