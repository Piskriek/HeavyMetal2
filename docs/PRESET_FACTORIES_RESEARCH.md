# Research: the Aq (species) and Lx (light) preset factories (Opus, 2026-10-21)

Plan 39 steps 3 and 4. The research-first SOP: how the closest games let players customise plants and lights, what players praise and complain about, and what the tech should be. Findings first, then what we take.

## 1. Lights: how builders let players set them

- **Satisfactory, Light Control Panel.** One panel sets every light wired to it: 7 colour slots (edited like the Customizer's colours), an intensity, a night-only mode and an on/off switch; one light's settings copy to another with Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V. [wiki: Ceiling Light](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Ceiling_Light), [Street Light](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Street_Light). The colour swatches are global: editing a swatch recolours everything painted with it, which players both rely on and get bitten by ("everything painted with that swatch change[s] color immediately"); there is no bulk recolour of already placed buildings without a mod. [Customizer](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Customizer), [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/526870/discussions/0/3421061982931861679), [CustomSwatches mod](https://www.mado-hosting.com/satisfactory-mods/CustomSwatches).
- **Space Engineers, Interior Light.** Per block: RGB colour, radius (about 1 to 20 m), falloff (0 to 3: 1 linear, 2 quadratic), intensity, offset, and blink interval (up to 30 s), length and offset. Players set them from the toolbar or a timer block, and ask for more of them to be scriptable; a community script saves and restores light profiles because the game has none. [wiki](https://spaceengineers.wiki.gg/wiki/Interior_Light), [feedback](https://support.keenswh.com/spaceengineers/pc/topic/48230-interior-light-action-set-blink-offset-set-blink-length), [Light Profiles script](https://github.com/illusori/space-engineers-zi-light-profiles), [Steam advice](https://steamcommunity.com/app/244850/discussions/0/558754899377273817).
- **No Man's Sky, base lights.** The complaints are about the look, not the controls: the falloff is "way too short", so lights act as harsh spotlights and indoor spaces stay dark; players mod the bloom and the indoor lighting. A construction limit caps parts for frame rate. [Steam threads](https://steamcommunity.com/app/275850/discussions/0/3874841853293896646), [mod](https://www.nexusmods.com/nomanssky/mods/2440).
- **Kelvin or RGB.** No player study compares them, but tools agree on the pattern: named presets first ("candlelight", "overcast"), a Kelvin slider for realistic warm-to-cool, RGB for stylised colour, and never both modes side by side (a smart-lighting user could not tell which mode was active). Keep the slider's direction consistent (warm on the left). [Substance Kelvin plugin](https://80.lv/articles/kelvin-color-for-substance-painter), [s&box lighting helpers](https://sbox.game/fwotsoft/lightinghelpers), [SharpTools thread](https://community.sharptools.io/t/color-temperature-improvements/4062), [Unreal thread](https://answers.unrealengine.com/questions/223925/view.html).

**What we take (Lx):** a light preset is a few numbers: a temperature in kelvin (or, as the advanced mode, a signal colour), an intensity, and a pattern (steady, slow pulse, blink, flicker). Named presets come first on the bench, with the Kelvin slider drawn as a warm-to-cool track; the signal colour is a separate mode, not shown beside it. One preset can be fired at one lamp or held to set every lamp on the machine (the Satisfactory panel's reach, without its global-swatch surprise: a lamp changes only when the player fires at it). Lamps glow by emissive material, so a preset costs nothing per frame; real point lights stay a Graphics-tier extra. The NMS lesson: generous falloff, never a dark interior.

## 2. Plants: how terraforming and garden games let players shape flora

- **The Planet Crafter.** Trees come from a DNA Manipulator (seed plus bark plus mutagen). Players asked for flower genetics that change "shapes, colors and sizes", seed cloning, and display pots for their collections. [Steam threads](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1284190/discussions/0/3275816470987915586), [seeds guide](https://dotesports.com/the-planet-crafter/news/how-to-get-seeds-in-the-planet-crafter).
- **Terra Nil.** A restoration game: 88 % positive for its calm and its "appreciate" mode that shows off the restored land; complaints are performance, a forced photo mode, and no sandbox. [review summary](https://vaporlens.app/app/1593030/terra_nil), [stats](https://www.steampulse.org/game/1593030).
- **Tiny Glade (2024).** Procedural detail (ivy, props) is the praise; the top complaint is too few trees, plants and decorations, and no copy and paste. [review](https://gamingonlinux.com/2024/06/tiny-glade-is-a-gorgeous-castle-doodling-game-thats-real-clever-to-relax-with), [developers](https://80.lv/articles/exclusive-tiny-glade-developers-discuss-bevy-proceduralism-publishers-cozy-games/).
- **Grow: Song of the Evertree.** Players loved crafting whole worlds from seeds whose essences set the plant life; the gardening chores were the complaint. [Nintendo World Report](https://www.nintendoworldreport.com/review/59017), [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1380420/discussions/0/4962398753461477133/?ctp=4).
- **The tech.** Instancing batches only instances that share a mesh and a material; per-instance colour, scale and wind phase add variety without breaking the batch. A tree with different branches is a different mesh, so it needs its own instanced set. [Unity GPU instancing](https://docs.unity3d.com/es/2020.1/Manual/GPUInstancing.html), [instancing deep dive](https://stefangroenewoud.artstation.com/blog/wBAPD/day-39-deep-dive-mesh-instancing).

**What we take (Aq):** a species preset is a species plus its sliders (height, crown spread, crown base, lean, branch density, leaf size, sway, canopy palette), shown as a tree growing in the bench's preview, with named presets first ("Alpine pine", "Autumn oak", "Hydro birch"). Fired at a tree, it regrows that tree; held, it regrows every tree of that tree's kind in reach (copy and paste for trees, which Tiny Glade players asked for). Each distinct preset on the plot is one instanced set (near and far), so a plot holds a few custom species, not one mesh per tree; the cap is said plainly when reached. No chores: a tree changes at once and stays changed (saved by its place).

## 3. Shared by both
- **Previews drive play** (the owner, 2026-10-20): each bench page shows the exact result live (a tree growing, a lamp glowing over a floor) and tries a choice on by hover.
- **Cost:** neither has a raw stock item (the game has raw pixels and raw vertices only), and a fifth metric is out (constitution 1). A species or light cartridge is written like a Texture Mill preset: a blank, bench time and power. A raw-light or raw-water stock is a later call for Argo.
- **Like for like:** a species cartridge fits trees, a light cartridge fits lamps.

## 4. Saving and sharing presets (for plan 39 section 7)
- **Satisfactory blueprints** live per player in local files, left out of cloud saves, and are shared by passing two files that must travel together; ratings live on community sites. ([guide](https://gamever.io/knowledge-base/how-to-share-blueprints-on-your-satisfactory-server), [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/526870/discussions/0/4845399912178942467))
- **Spore** stored each creation inside its own PNG picture (about 8 KB of data hidden in the image), so a creation could travel anywhere an image could; 100 million were uploaded to the Sporepedia by May 2009. ([Ned Batchelder](https://nedbatchelder.com/blog/200806/spore_creature_creator_and_steganography), [EA](https://www.ea.com/news/100-million-creatures-take-over-spore-universe))
- **Space Engineers blueprints** break for a recipient who lacks a DLC or mod ("missing blocks"), and projections misalign after updates. ([wiki](https://spaceengineers.wiki.gg/wiki/Blueprint), [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/244850/discussions/0/2243301553218556336))
- **No Man's Sky's** palette: "the color you see in the color palette will look completely different on your character's armor"; the preview lied. ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/275850/discussions/0/3758850305396539744/?l=latam))

**What we take:** a player's presets live in the profile with the game ("Yours", after the named ones on every bench and in the Studio), shared as one short code (and later, Spore-style, as a picture); a preset only ever names base generators, materials and species everyone has, and is checked on load, so it never arrives with missing pieces; previews keep using the game's own build calls, so what the player saves is what the world shows.
