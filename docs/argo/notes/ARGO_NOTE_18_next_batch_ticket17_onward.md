## **1\. Executive Summary & How We Solve the Two Main Constraints**

With TICKET-09 through TICKET-16 delivered and queued, all core campaign acts (Acts I–IX), the 144 BPM ending credits video, the 5-bus audio engine, the harmonic choir, and the heavy terraformers/turrets are built. Two hard engineering constraints govern everything from TICKET-17 onward:

### **Constraint A — The Single-File Build Budget (15.26 MiB / 16.00 MiB Cap \= \~0.74 MiB Left)**

Instead of rationing our last 0.74 MiB, **TICKET-17 reclaims \~1.38 MiB (-1,410 KB)** from dist/index.html right away, dropping the single-file build from 15.26 MiB down to **\~13.88 MiB** (giving us **\> 2.1 MiB of safe headroom** below the 16.00 MiB gate):

> 1. **Move the 6 Vista-Only Architectural low.glb Models to public/hd/vista/ (Saves \~520 KB inline):** Per POL-27 (*“Next room if needed: vista-only props to a fetched side file behind the loading bar”*), datacentre-hall, chiller-block, shipping-container, pylon-tower, overpass-segment, and rubble-slab are only ever drawn in the Full and Lite 3D Lab window views—never on the Moon plot and never in the Static window view. Moving their low.glb files from inline src/assets/gen/ to public/hd/vista/ (fetched asynchronously in preloadLabGenerated(), while the 0-byte Static shader plane covers file:// offline opens) removes \~520 KB from dist/index.html.  
> 2. **Move Unreferenced Overnight-Batch Scenery Lows Out of Inline import.meta.glob (Saves \~380 KB inline):** The registry holds 128 packed assets, including large overnight-batch ruin props (office-block-ruin, datacentre-annex, silo-square, comms-mast-solid, portable-office, checkpoint-booth) that are not in NEIGHBOUR\_MODELS (wreck-sites.ts) or lab-room.ts. Moving unreferenced scenery low.glb files to public/hd/scenery/ (loaded on demand when placed in the Studio Scene tab) saves \~380 KB.  
> 3. **Apply KHR\_mesh\_quantization \+ webp(78) to Remaining Inline low.glb Cages in runworld-pack.mjs (Saves \~450 KB inline):** Quantizing vertex positions (14-bit), normals (10-bit), and UVs (12-bit) and encoding the 256×256 baked low textures as WebP (quality 78\) shrinks the remaining inline low.glb payloads by \~22% (\~450 KB) with zero silhouette or luminance loss.  
> 4. **Deduplicate Embedded CSS Strings (POL-87, POL-93) (Saves \~60 KB inline):** Moving pure helpers out of planetary-grid.tsx, audio-tab.tsx, look-tab.tsx, and scene-tab.tsx so their stylesheets are imported once rather than duplicated as inline TS template strings saves \~60 KB.

### **Constraint B — The play-scene.ts Single-Writer Queue**

While the current play-scene.ts queue finishes installing T13b, T16b (including POL-301..304 and POL-141), T09b, T07b, F-13, and T15:

> * **4 of the 6 new tickets (TICKET-17, TICKET-18, TICKET-20, TICKET-21) touch ZERO lines of play-scene.ts** and can be dispatched immediately in parallel across Haiku and Flash sidecars.  
> * All remaining play-scene.ts changes across the entire project are consolidated into just **two self-contained hook modules** (**Window F** in TICKET-19 and **Window G** in TICKET-22), keeping play-scene.ts edits under 15 lines per window.
