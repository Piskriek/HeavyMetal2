# 26b — "Four Floats" v2: the rhythm rewrite the owner liked

> **Author:** Claude Opus, from Argo's plan 26 (his concept, story and images), after the owner's review of v1.
> **Date:** 14 October 2026
> **Status:** v2 is the ending and credits song (`apps/web/public/audio/music-ending.mp3`, from `zips/runworld/music/four-floats-v2.mp3`). The owner: "i like it, its a banger".

## Why v2
The owner on v1: "it feels like its missing a beat and 8bit background music and the words feel too accurate, the singer is having to force a rythm with words that dont fit that shape".

v2 keeps Argo's story and jokes, and changes three things:
- **A strict, singable meter:** verses alternate 5- and 7-syllable lines, with every stress on a beat; the bridge is even 6-syllable lines.
- **A repeating hook:** "Four floats in the dark".
- **The beat:** a drum machine from bar one, with 8-bit arpeggios and a pulse bass under the whole song. The arrangement still climbs the fidelity ladder.

**Still to do (the owner):** "it ends on the words sector 4 and just stops, i recomend popping in the courus with no vocals after that and loop it".
