Cables run in low ribbed steel floor covers or wall conduits. NO chimneys, NO stacks, NO smokestacks, NO exhaust pipes. No lettering on any surface. Review each S1/S6 pair side by side before keeping it.

A POWER cable (black) and a DATA cable (grey) run from the base in a low ribbed steel floor cover and rise into a vertical WALL CONDUIT on the wall behind the unit. A hazmat scientist, 1.8 metres tall, stands beside the unit for scale. No pixels of any colour: the unit emits nothing; no pipes going up, no chimney, no stack.
