ROOM (every 21 panel; the room is one shared scene):
A pristine quantum-shielded white-room laboratory, about 16 by 12 metres, with a 5 metre ceiling. NORTH WALL: one wide observation WINDOW, full width, floor to about 3.5 metres, thick framed glass. Outside the window: the void storm, reality running out of compute: dithered black-and-white static clouds and broken pixel lightning. CENTRE-SOUTH: THE GATE, a chunky steel door-frame gate standing on a raised round plinth with two steps, its opening facing the window so the gate frames the storm; ribbed amber coil blocks on its frame; thick power cables run to its plinth in low ribbed floor covers. WEST WALL, THE POWER WALL, read left to right: a capacitor bank, a breaker panel, four relay cabinets, two control boxes, then the operator console with a big throw lever facing into the room; one cable tray runs along the wall carrying the cables from the capacitors to the gate. EAST SIDE, THE STUDIO CORNER: a preset bench, a preset combiner, a preset rack of cartridges against the east wall, and in front of them a round planet table showing a small amber hologram of the Moon's plots (glowing abstract plots only, no text). SOUTH-EAST NOOK, THE ARMORY: an armory rack on the wall holding an extraction beam tool, and the weapon bench (sheet 20) beside it. SOUTH-WEST CORNER, WHERE SHE LIVES: a scientist's desk with stacked notes and a coffee mug, a folding cot with a blanket, readings taped to a wall panel, and a radio grille in the console.
