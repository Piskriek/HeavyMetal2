DEFINITIONS (hidden tests check them exactly):
- slopeDeg: central differences ((h[x+1]-h[x-1])/(2*cellM), the same in z), one-sided at the grid edges, as atan(|gradient|) in degrees.
- A (wet) = 255 on water cells. Elsewhere it is round(255*max(0,1-d/WET_REACH)), where d is the Euclidean metres from the cell centre to the nearest water cell centre. It is independent of stage.
- stage<2: G=B=0 everywhere and scatter=[].
- G and B never decrease, and R never increases, as stage grows (inputs otherwise equal). G and B also never decrease as aq or lx grow. G=B=0 on water cells. B=0 where slope>35; G=0 where slope>45.
- Each Scatter has appear in [FIRST_STAGE[kind],6], and is present iff appear<=stage. Its fields are identical at every stage where it is present (monotone growth: raising the stage only adds instances).
- Never place scatter on a water cell, a cell with slope>35, within r of a machine, inside a keep-out, or outside [0,n*cellM).
- Any two instances are at least max(SPACING[a],SPACING[b]) apart.
- Trees go only on cells where G or B would be >=128 at stage 6.
- Caps: at most CAPS[tier] instances. Choose the instances in an order that keeps monotone growth under the cap: sort by appear, ties by a seeded hash, then take a prefix.
- scale in [0.7,1.3], yaw in [0,2*PI).
- Tufts appear first nearest the machines (appear rises with distance). Bushes favour wet cells. Pines go on drier, higher ground, birches near water, oaks in between.
- No NaN. The same input gives a byte-identical cover and a deep-equal scatter. A different seed gives a different scatter.
- Speed: n=256 in under 40 ms in Node.
