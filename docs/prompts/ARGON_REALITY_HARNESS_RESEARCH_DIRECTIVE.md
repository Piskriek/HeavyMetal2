## 2. FRONTIER RESEARCH DOMAINS TO INVESTIGATE DEEPLY

Investigate cutting-edge research across industry labs (NVIDIA Research, Meta FAIR, DeepMind, academic differential geometry) and determine how their mathematical cores can be adapted to our pure-function, deterministic TypeScript/WebGL2 engine.

### Domain 1: NVIDIA MotionBricks (SIGGRAPH 2026 / NVlabs) & Latent Smart Primitives
- **Background**: NVIDIA just released **MotionBricks** (`NVlabs/motionbricks`), replacing traditional game animation graphs (fragile state machines with thousands of hand-crafted transitions) with a single continuous latent neural network backbone trained on 350,000+ motion clips. It runs at **15,000 FPS with ~2ms latency**, using modular **"Smart Primitives"** that compose navigation, styles, and keyframe targets in a plug-and-play manner without retraining.
- **Your Research Questions**:
  1. How does MotionBricks mathematically parameterize continuous motion phase spaces without state-machine edge explosion?
  2. How can we adapt the principles of "Smart Primitives" into our `@hm/anim`, `@hm/puppet`, and `@hm/kinematic` packages?
  3. Can we build a **micro-latent motion manifold** or an analytical phase-variable generator that achieves MotionBricks-style composability in a browser with `< 100 KB` of deterministic code (or a quantized ONNX micro-model) without requiring multi-gigabyte neural weights?
  4. How does motion scale across our 4-float formula $\mathbf{S}=(\text{Pxd}, \text{Vtx}, \text{Lx}, \text{Aq})$ and the 7 stages (from discrete 8Hz keyframed retro ticks at $S_0$ to continuous physical momentum at $S_6$)?

### Domain 2: Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA / V-JEPA) for System-1 Perception
- **Background**: Autoregressive LLMs (predicting the next token) are slow (500–2000 ms), expensive, and hallucinate continuous coordinate math. Yann LeCun's **JEPA** (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture) predicts representations in an abstract latent space using energy functions rather than predicting raw pixels or text tokens.
- **Your Research Questions**:
  1. How can the "Artificial Ego" be formulated as an **Energy-Based Model (EBM)** or non-autoregressive latent transducer?
  2. How does an energy function $E(x, y)$ evaluate whether an incoming external 3D entity $x$ is topologically and physically compatible with a 5-Group target kit $y$ (`base`, `structure`, `mechanism`, `controls`, `subject`) in $< 2\text{ ms}$?
  3. How does System 1 communicate with System 2 (Claude Opus 5.5)? What is the exact data structure of the **Latent Vector Shorthand** that allows Claude to reason about high-level mechanics without getting bogged down in vertex arrays?

### Domain 3: Spectral Manifold Geometry, Laplace-Beltrami Operators & Functional Maps
- **Background**: Traditional 3D importing requires manual rigging, UV unwrapping, and vertex weighting. In spectral geometry (Ovsjanikov, Rustamov, Guibas), the **Laplace-Beltrami Operator** ($\Delta_{\mathcal{M}} \psi_i = \lambda_i \psi_i$) yields intrinsic geometric frequencies (eigenfunctions) that are invariant to isometric deformation (bending, rotation, scale). **Heat Kernel Signatures (HKS)** and **Wave Kernel Signatures (WKS)** identify topological landmarks (limbs, heads, hubs) purely from intrinsic mesh curvature.
- **Your Research Questions**:
  1. Can we run a discrete Laplace-Beltrami cotangent Laplacian on incoming arbitrary 3D meshes inside a Web Worker?
  2. Can the first $k$ eigenfunctions ($k \approx 30$) automatically segment an incoming mesh into our 5 anatomical groups (`base`, `structure`, `mechanism`, `controls`, `subject`) and locate socket triads without human intervention?
  3. How do **Functional Maps** allow transferring motion presets and material stacks from our Studio onto arbitrary imported meshes via a simple small linear matrix transformation ($C \in \mathbb{R}^{k \times k}$)?

### Domain 4: Vector Symbolic Architectures (VSA) / Hyperdimensional Computing (HDC)
- **Background**: Kanerva's Hyperdimensional Computing and Vector Symbolic Architectures (Holographic Reduced Representations) use high-dimensional vectors ($D \approx 1024$ or $2048$) with algebraic operations: **Binding** ($\otimes$, circular convolution or XOR), **Bundling / Superposition** ($\oplus$, vector addition), and **Permutation** ($\Pi$).
- **Your Research Questions**:
  1. Can our 5 Rungs of Presets be encoded as Hyperdimensional Vectors?
  2. Can the user's "Ego Filter" (translating an incoming digital world into a different genre or style) be executed as pure vector algebra:
     $$\mathbf{V}_{\text{Rendered}} = \mathbf{V}_{\text{RawWorld}} \otimes \mathbf{V}_{\text{StyleEgo}}^{-1}$$
  3. How can VSA associative clean-up memories map continuous latent outputs back to discrete Studio presets with zero inference latency and zero floating-point drift?

### Domain 5: Conformal Geometric Algebra ($\mathcal{G}_{4,1}$ CGA) for Kinematic Invariants
- **Background**: Standard game engines use $4\times 4$ homogeneous matrices and quaternions, leading to gimbal lock, scale shear, and cumbersome dual-quaternion blending. Conformal Geometric Algebra ($\mathcal{G}_{4,1}$) represents points, lines, planes, circles, spheres, rotations, translations, and dilations uniformly as **multivector motors**.
- **Your Research Questions**:
  1. Can we replace standard matrix cascades in `@hm/kinematic` and two-bone IK with CGA motors?
  2. How does CGA simplify universal socket snapping, beam raycasting, and cross-Stargate spatial transforms?

### Domain 6: 2D Gaussian Surfels & Multi-Resolution Neural Parametric Surfaces
- **Background**: Traditional games use static polygonal LOD meshes. Modern frontier graphics combines oriented 2D tangent-disk Gaussian surfels (normal-oriented disks) with hierarchical multi-resolution hash encodings (Instant-NGP / NanoVDB).
- **Your Research Questions**:
  1. How do 2D Gaussian surfels unify terrain contact skirts, decals, and micro-geometry?
  2. How does our 4-float formula $\mathbf{S}=(\text{Pxd}, \text{Vtx}, \text{Lx}, \text{Aq})$ smoothly dial surfel splat size and density between Stage $S_0$ (sparse 1-bit points) and Stage $S_6$ (dense anisotropic PBR surfels) without sorting artifacts?
