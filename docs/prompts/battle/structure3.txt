- v is 1; at most maxStructures structures and maxPieces pieces; every id (structures and pieces share one id space) is a non-negative integer, unique, and below nextId; nextId <= 2^31.
- structure x, y, z finite with |value| <= coord; yaw finite in [-PI, PI].
