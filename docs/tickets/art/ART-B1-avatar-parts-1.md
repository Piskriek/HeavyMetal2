# ART-B1: Painted goblin parts, part 1 (30 images)

- **Batch**: agent 1 of 4 in art wave 1 (PR branch `art/generated-wave-1`; the other three run at
  the same time). Read [README.md](README.md) first. ART-B2 also writes
  `painted-parts.generated.ts`: follow the conflict rule in the README.
- **Feeds**: the Goblin Creator (`src/components/creator/CharacterCreatorStudio.tsx`) through
  [ART-I1](ART-I1-painted-parts-dna-v3.md).
- **Why**: the creator's six painted parts point at PNGs that were never delivered (the dev server
  returns its HTML page instead), and every other part is flat vector art. Plan target: ~60 painted
  parts (docs/multiplayer-plan/content/plan/09-avatar-png-pipeline.md §9.7).

## Rules for this batch
- Every image: **Raw** `art-src/avatar-parts/raw/<id>.png`, then
  `node --import tsx scripts/key-art.ts --set avatar-parts --only <id>` must print PASS.
- It writes `public/avatar-parts/keyed/<id>.png` and updates `src/game/meta/painted-parts.generated.ts`.
  Commit both.
- Parts with painted skin (ears, noses, lips) are painted in the toxic green `#7fb24a` family on
  purpose: ART-I1 builds tint masks from that hue so they follow the skin swatch.
- **Do not** edit `AVATAR_CATALOG` or `PAINTED_PARTS`: registering new parts changes the DNA radix
  and is ART-I1's job (DNA v3).
- Pivot notes are for ART-I1; you don't need to act on them.
