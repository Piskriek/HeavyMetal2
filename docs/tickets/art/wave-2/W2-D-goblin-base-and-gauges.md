# W2-D: The last vector goblin parts, and painted needles and gauges (12 images, 1 depth map)

- **Batch**: agent 5 of 7 in art wave 2 (branch `art/generated-wave-2`; the others run at the same time).
  Read [README.md](README.md) first: its prompt, review loop and depth-map steps are binding.
- **Feeds**: the owner's rule that nothing stays vector-drawn. These replace the creator's three SVG head shapes, its four SVG war paints and the SVG "goggles up", and the SVG gauge needles.

## Where these are seen (design notes)
The Goblin Creator shows each part four ways: on the goblin in the big arched mirror (up to 470 px), alone
in a tray well cropped tight to the part (about 100 px), in a round 46 px medallion, and on the finished
goblin at 36–60 px in races. So every part needs **one clear idea and a silhouette that reads at 48 px**,
and it must look like a sibling of the wave-1 parts, painted by the same hand. Detail finer than the
outline weight disappears; spend the detail on the shape instead. Hue matters: the tint masks recolour
skin green (#7fb24a family), dark brown leather, brass and neutral red, so paint those materials in exactly
those colours.

**Heads** are the canvas every other part is painted onto: they must stay blank, evenly lit and the same green
as the painted ears and eyes (pass both references). **War paints** replace the four SVG ones and sit where
those sat; they are paint on skin, not stickers, so they have no outlines. **Needles and the gauge face**
are read at a glance mid-race: a clean, bold silhouette and a hub exactly on the centre line.

## Rules for this batch
- Every part: **Raw** `art-src/avatar-parts/raw/<id>.png`, then
  `node --import tsx scripts/key-art.ts --set avatar-parts --only <id>` must print PASS. It writes the keyed PNG,
  runs the edge repair itself and updates `src/game/meta/painted-parts.generated.ts`. Commit all three.
- Pass the image's **Reference** file as the image input for every try (it sets the style, not the subject).
- Run the README's **review loop** after keying. Do **not** edit `AVATAR_CATALOG` or `PAINTED_PARTS`.
- Ears: paint ONE ear, a **left** ear, its root against the right edge (the creator mirrors it).
- **Depth maps** (parts marked "Depth map"): after the part passes, paint its depth map with the README's
  depth-map steps, then `node scripts/build-depth-mask.mjs --only <id>` must print PASS. Commit the map raw
  (`art-src/avatar-parts/depth-raw/<id>.png`) and `public/avatar-parts/depth/<id>.png`.
- **Heads must be blank**: regenerate any head that paints an eye, brow, nose, mouth, ear or hair.
- War paint (4–7): **no reference image** and no ink outlines. W2-A2 paints two more with the same wording.
- Images 9–12: the listed process. Needles point straight **up** with the hub at the bottom centre
  (the game rotates them about the hub).
