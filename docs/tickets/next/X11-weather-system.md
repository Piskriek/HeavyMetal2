# X11: Dynamic Track Weather and Environmental Hazards

- **ID**: `X11`
- **Priority**: Creative Proposal
- **Component**: Environmental System / Weather Shaders / Friction Physics
- **Conflicts with**: None
- **Needs art**: No

---

## Proposal
Introduce randomized or track-selected dynamic weather phenomena affecting both atmospheric visuals and road handling. Copperwood Ridge can experience torrential highland rain that creates slick mud slicks and reduces tire traction, Boomtown Quarry can suffer blinding ember squalls that swirl glowing volcanic ash and impair long-range visibility, and Woolly Wasteland can be blanketed by dense mountain fog banks with howling winds that push airborne marbles sideways. Weather transitions seamlessly between stages and provides distinctive atmospheric identity to repeat tournament runs.

---

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] At least three distinct weather variants (Highland Rain, Ember Storm, Mountain Fog) can be triggered per course.
- [ ] Rain slicks reduce lateral grip and increase braking distance; wind gusts apply lateral force during airborne jumps.
- [ ] Particle systems (rain streaks, flying embers, rolling fog volume) render at full 60 FPS.
