# X13: Marble Capsule Customization — Paint, Decals, and Tire Rims

- **ID**: `X13`
- **Priority**: Creative Proposal
- **Component**: Customization / Workshop / Texture Synthesis
- **Conflicts with**: None
- **Needs art**: Yes (Decal sheets with painted flames, goblin skulls, racing stripes, and check patterns)

---

## Proposal
Expand the pre-race Workshop stage to support visual customization of the player's racing marble. Players can select primary and secondary lacquer paints (metallic bronze, rusted iron, molten gold, poison green, matte coal), apply unlocked painted vinyl decals (hot rod flames, goblin skull emblems, checkered racing stripes, crossbones), and equip custom wheel hubcaps (spiked brass spinners, sawblade rims, iron cog gears). Custom liveries are dynamically baked into the marble canvas texture and visible in both 3D third-person race view and the character showcase.

---

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Workshop includes a "Paint & Livery" customization tab for color swatches, decals, and hubcap styles.
- [ ] Customized liveries bake directly onto the 3D marble texture canvas and render in-game.
- [ ] Custom schemes persist in player local storage across sessions.
