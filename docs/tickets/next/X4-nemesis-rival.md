# X4: Dynamic Nemesis Rival System

- **ID**: `X4`
- **Priority**: Creative Proposal
- **Component**: AI Gameplay / Narrative / Tournament Rivalry
- **Conflicts with**: None
- **Needs art**: No

---

## Proposal
Introduce a dynamic "Nemesis Rival" mechanic across tournament rounds. Based on qualifying times and early race contact, the AI driver closest in overall championship points is designated as the player's personal Nemesis for the round. The Nemesis features distinct visual badging (a spiked red target icon over their marble), aggressive tactical AI that prioritizes blocking the player's line, and custom radio taunts when bumping or overtaking the player. Defeating your Nemesis awards bonus scrap bounty and unlocks unique loadout decals.

---

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] At the start of a tournament race, the closest championship rival is selected and badged as the "Nemesis".
- [ ] The Nemesis marble displays a distinct menacing crown/spikes icon on HUD track maps and off-screen pointers.
- [ ] Finishing ahead of the Nemesis grants bonus championship scrap bounty and custom victory dialogue.
