# X5: Goblin Pit Crew Radio Chatter and Dynamic Voice Barks

- **ID**: `X5`
- **Priority**: Creative Proposal
- **Component**: Audio / Voice Barks / Narrative Flavor
- **Conflicts with**: None
- **Needs art**: No

---

## Proposal
Add a dynamic goblin pit crew radio chatter system that triggers stylized text and voice barks during key race events. An animated pit crew goblin portrait appears in the top-left HUD with crackling radio static audio when significant milestones occur—such as surviving a heavy TNT explosion ("THAT WAS SUPPOSED TO DETONATE BEHIND YOU!"), nailing a massive cliff jump ("DON'T LOOK DOWN, LOOK AT THE TROPHY!"), entering the first split alone ("SPLIT ONE IS ALL YOU, CHIEF!"), or burning a triple nitro boost ("THE ENGINE IS MELTING! PERFECT!"). Voice barks include toggleable frequency settings (frequent, tactical-only, muted) and full subtitle support.

---

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Radio transmission HUD toast appears with crackle sound effect on key milestones (air jumps, TNT hits, split crossings).
- [ ] Includes at least 25 unique humorous goblin crew lines.
- [ ] Subtitles and voice toggle settings are accessible in the Settings menu.
