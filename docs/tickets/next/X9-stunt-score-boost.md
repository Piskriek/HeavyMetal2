# X9: Stunt Scoring and Dynamic Nitro Meter Refill

- **ID**: `X9`
- **Priority**: Creative Proposal
- **Component**: Scoring System / Nitro Mechanics / Player Agency
- **Conflicts with**: None
- **Needs art**: No

---

## Proposal
Transform mid-air jumps, hair-raising near-misses, and multi-marble bounce combos into actionable speed resources. When the player performs dangerous driving maneuvers—such as spending $>1.5$ seconds airborne off a cliff, executing multiple chain hops across consecutive springboards, or sideswiping three rivals in a single corner—a floating arcade stunt score multiplier appears (`"AIR TIME! +250"`, `"NEAR MISS! x2"`). Filling the stunt combo meter awards an immediate free Nitro Turbo Boost charge, rewarding high-risk aggressive driving and offering comeback opportunities when running low on trackside pickups.

---

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Sustained air time ($>1.2\text{ s}$) and consecutive obstacle hops accumulate visible stunt combo points on screen.
- [ ] Filling the 1,000-point stunt meter instantly rewards the player with 1 extra Nitro Boost charge.
- [ ] Stunt toasts feature golden arcade popups with sound stingers without obscuring forward visibility.
